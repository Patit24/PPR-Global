- **Heading:** *Tell us about your practice*

  - Name
  - Phone (WhatsApp preferred)
  - Clinic or hospital name
  - Type of practice (Dropdown: Single-doctor clinic, Polyclinic, Dental clinic, Diagnostic lab, Hospital, Wellness / Ayurveda, Other)
  - What do you need? (Website / Mobile app / WhatsApp automation / Patient leads / Not sure)
- **Submit Button:** *Get My Free Consultation*
- **Consent Notice:** *I agree to be contacted by PPR Global about my enquiry. We will never share your details.*
- **Success Message:** *Thank you. We will contact you within one business day.*

### Floating WhatsApp Button
- **Label:** *Chat with us*
- **Pre-filled Message:** *Hi, I run a healthcare practice and want to know about a website or app.*

- *Get a free review of your clinic's website and Google presence. [Claim My Free Review]*

### Lead Magnet (Free Download)
- **Title:** *The Clinic Owner's Checklist: 15 Things Your Website Needs to Get More Patients*
