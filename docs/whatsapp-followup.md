# WhatsApp Auto-Reply & Follow-Up Automation Architecture

This specification outlines the 3-step conversational lead qualification and follow-up architecture designed for **PPR Global** (`pprglobal.online`).

---

## 1. Overview & Triggers
When a visitor submits an inquiry via `<LeadForm />`, `#calculator` ("Email Me This Estimate"), or taps a WhatsApp CTA, their lead record is saved in `/api/leads`.

The payload dispatched to your CRM or WhatsApp Business API provider (such as Twilio, Wati, AiSensy, or Meta Cloud API) triggers the automated messaging sequences below.

---

## 2. Required Data Fields from `/api/leads`

The automation engine expects the following fields generated by `/api/leads`:

| Field Name | Type | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `name` | string | Customer full name (e.g., "Dr. Shaheen", "Amit") |
| `phone` | string | Normalized phone number with country code (e.g., `+919876543210`) |
| `email` | string \| null | Optional contact email address |
| `service` | string | Selected service (e.g. "Website Development", "Free Website & GBP Review") |
| `budget` | string | Selected price range (e.g. "$300–$600 / ₹28,000–₹57,000") |
| `source` | string | Origin tag (`contact`, `free-audit`, `calculator_estimate`, `service_slug`) |
| `message` | string | Customer project notes, questions, or estimated breakdown |
| `leadId` | string | Unique identifier for tracking and CRM deduplication |

---

## 3. Conversational 3-Step Sequence

### Step 1: Instant Auto-Reply (< 60 Seconds)
**Trigger**: Dispatched immediately upon receiving `POST /api/leads` with a valid `phone`.  
**Goal**: Confirm receipt instantly, establish agency authority, and capture project scope (business type, budget, timeline).

```text
Hi {{name}}! 👋 Thanks for reaching out to PPR Global.

I'm Patit Roy (Founder & Lead Developer). We received your inquiry regarding {{service}}.

To help us prepare your custom roadmap and exact estimate before our quick chat, could you reply with:
1️⃣ Your business type & current website (if any)?
2️⃣ Your ideal launch timeline (e.g., Live in 7 days, or within a month)?
3️⃣ Any specific features you need (e.g., WhatsApp booking, payment gateway, SEO)?

I'll review this and send back a personalized breakdown shortly!
```

---

### Step 2: Follow-Up 1 (1 Hour Post-Inquiry)
**Condition**: Sent if the customer hasn't replied to Step 1.  
**Goal**: Gentle nudge showing founder attentiveness + quick social proof.

```text
Hey {{name}}, just following up to make sure you saw my previous message!

We recently helped a local clinic increase appointments by 65% and launched an interior studio website in under 2 weeks.

Are you available for a quick 5-minute call or WhatsApp chat later today to discuss {{service}}?
```

---

### Step 3: Follow-Up 2 (24 Hours / Day 1)
**Condition**: Sent at 24 hours if no reply is logged.  
**Goal**: Value-add offering a complimentary layout wireframe or free audit report.

```text
Hi {{name}}, Patit from PPR Global here.

I took a quick look at project requirements for {{service}}. I’d love to put together a free 10-minute strategy overview and preliminary wireframe for you—no obligation at all.

Would you like me to send that over to your WhatsApp or email?
```

---

### Step 4: Final Check-in (72 Hours / Day 3)
**Condition**: Sent on Day 3 if still unresponsive.  
**Goal**: Respectful permission-close / break-up message that leaves the door open without being pushy.

```text
Hi {{name}}, I know you're busy running your business, so I won't crowd your inbox.

If you decide to move forward with {{service}} or want to explore our "Live in 7 Days" fast-track build ($300+ / ₹28,750+), feel free to message me anytime right here.

Wishing you great success with your business!
— Patit Roy, PPR Global (https://www.pprglobal.online)
```

---

## 4. Implementation Stack Recommendation
- **Webhook Provider**: Set `CRM_WEBHOOK_URL` in `.env.local` to trigger a Zapier, Make.com, or n8n workflow.
- **WhatsApp Provider**: Connect to WhatsApp Cloud API, Wati, or Interakt using the webhook payload.
- **State Tracking**: Update `lead_status` in Supabase (`contacted` -> `qualified` -> `won`) when a reply is detected.
